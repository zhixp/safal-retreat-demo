Angan Lawn

The largest thing on the property and the reason most weddings choose it. Open expanses of grass under the sky during the day, and under whatever you hang from the poles at night.

The lake sits past the tree line, so the light at the end of the afternoon comes off the water and across the grass. It is the hour the photographs are taken in.

The Banquet Hall

Pillarless, which is a plain word for the thing that decides whether a reception works. No column stands between a table and the stage, so the room seats what the number says and every guest has the front of it.

It takes a sit-down dinner, a conference, a product launch or a reception that has moved indoors because the weather changed its mind.
