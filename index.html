Built for the lake it sits on

Safal Retreat is a boutique property on the Prempura stretch of Bhopal's Upper Lake. The rooms face the water. The lawns run to the bank. The restaurant cooks the food of this region rather than a version of it assembled for guests.

It is small on purpose. Thirty rooms, three event spaces, one kitchen, and a team that can hold all of it in mind at once.
